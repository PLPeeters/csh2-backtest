# Belgian savings and CSH2 backtest

This context defines the financial terms used when comparing Belgian regulated savings with CSH2 over dated cash flows.

## Language

**Fidelity premium acquired**:
The fidelity-premium entitlement earned when a deposit remains uninterrupted for the qualifying period, before it is credited to the account balance.
_Avoid_: Vested, paid

**Fidelity premium credited**:
A previously acquired fidelity-premium entitlement that has been added to the savings account balance on its credit date.
_Avoid_: Acquired, earned

**Accrued base interest**:
Base-rate interest earned since the last annual credit date that has not yet been added to the savings account balance.
_Avoid_: Paid interest, credited interest

**Economic value**:
The value of the savings account including its balance and interest entitlements that have been earned but not yet credited.
_Avoid_: Ending balance
